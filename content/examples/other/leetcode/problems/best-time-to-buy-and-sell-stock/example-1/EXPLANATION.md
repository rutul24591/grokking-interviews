# Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock — Example 1 (Less Optimized: Brute Force)

LeetCode: https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/

## Approach
All buy/sell pairs.

## Complexity (step-by-step)
1. Try buy day i and sell day j>i: O(n^2) differences.
2. Track maximum profit.
## Overall Complexity
- Time: O(n^2)
- Space: O(1)
